# Contributing to E.D.I.T.H.

Thanks for your interest in the project. EDITH is currently a research-stage hardware and software concept.

## Before contributing

- Open a GitHub Issue first and explain your proposal, expected benefit, and any relevant references or measurements.
- For hardware proposals, include dimensions, tolerances, component identifiers, and the intended validation method.
- For optics, distinguish simulations and hypotheses from measured optical performance. Do not claim that prototype lenses are safe prescription eyewear.
- Do not commit secrets, personal information, confidential manufacturer documents, or third-party proprietary schematics.
- Keep pull requests focused, describe testing performed, and disclose untested assumptions.

## Suggested issue categories

- Mechanical fit, ergonomics, hinges, and serviceability.
- Optical engineering and adaptive-vision feasibility.
- Electronics, PCB, power, RF, and thermal constraints.
- Embedded software, AR, privacy, and accessibility.
- Documentation, renders, and test evidence.

Please keep discussion constructive and technically grounded.
